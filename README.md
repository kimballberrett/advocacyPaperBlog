# Advocacy blog post: setup notes

## Files
- `index.html`: the page, with your draft text pasted in exactly as written.
- `style.css`: the design (drop cap, dark-mode toggle, phone layout).
- `images/figure1.png`, `images/figure2.png`: your two figures.

## Preview locally
Double-click `index.html` to open it in your browser. Check it on a phone-width window too.

## Before you publish
1. If you change your text, send me the updated .docx so the page matches it exactly.
2. Check the Sources list at the bottom and click each link once.
3. Decide whether you want the subtitle or author photo options (not included by default).

## Publish on GitHub Pages
1. Create a free GitHub account if needed, then click **New repository**. Name it something like `language-learning-blog`, set it to **Public**.
2. Click **uploading an existing file**, drag in `index.html`, `style.css`, and the `images` folder, then **Commit changes**.
3. Go to **Settings > Pages**. Under **Build and deployment**, set Source to **Deploy from a branch**, choose branch `main` and folder `/ (root)`, then **Save**.
4. Wait about a minute. Your link will look like `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/language-learning-blog/`.
5. Open the link on your phone before sending it.

## Delivery (rubric: 10 points)
Email the link to your friends and BCC your instructor. Keep the sent email.
